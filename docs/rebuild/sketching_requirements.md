# Sketching: the requirements

Status: **a draft, `ai-unreviewed`** (`in_1680`: the human asked for the properties of the sketching, which he found mostly good, to be written down, replacing the old rows K1 to K14 and K28 to K31, so that tests can then pin them). Each row says in words a property that must survive; its source is what the human said (`in_…`) or the decision it rests on. **Nothing here is approved until the human says so, row by row (`in_1444`, `in_1615`):** approve, cut or reword; only an approved row gets a test (`[S12] …`). How it looks (sizes, exact colours, the icons' drawing, where a bubble stands within a few pixels) is free to change and is not here.

## Contracts
- `Sketch` (`src/interaction/drawing.ts`): what a click does to a sketch's definition, pure, solved by the engine's solver; `src/sketchcontrol.ts`, `src/sketchcore.ts`, `src/sketchdrag.ts` the controller.
- `Marks` (`src/interaction/marks.ts`): which marks and offers show for a selection, ranked, with what they would move, pure.
- `Probe` (`src/engine/probe.ts`): what is within reach of holding, and what would move, pure.
- `MarkDraw` (`src/markdraw.ts`): the bubbles on the page, their places, their drags and their fading.
- `Intents` (`src/input.ts`): `click` with its count, `menu`.
- `ViewState` (`src/view.ts`): `lookAt`, `lookAlong`.
- `Drawing` (`src/world.ts`): `lines`.

## Inputs this section claims
- mouse: left click with a sketch tool on; double click with a sketch tool on; right click with a sketch tool on
- keys: escape ends the figure or the tool
