# Sheets: the cross section plane as a generic plane

`in_459` asked for the cross section plane to be a generic plane, translucent grey like the world panes, with one icon that goes through its modes, an invisible mode, a face to sketch on, a push / pull arrow that moves it, a move icon, and several planes cutting at once; the AI wrote its reading and order as a question and the human said "Go ahead" (`in_460`) with its defaults.

**Built (`in_460`):** a *sheet* is a plane part with `section` (kept as the flag; the cutting is no longer what it is, only one of its modes). **Modes** (`modeOf`, `Plane.cut / flip / hidden`): *cuts the half its normal points to*, *cuts the other half*, *does not cut*, *invisible*; one icon on each sheet goes through them with each press (`sheet:mode:<ref>`, one step, saved with the part, undo takes it back). **Several cutting:** `cutsNow` lists every sheet that cuts; the view clips by all of them (each sheet's cut face is stenciled on its own and cut by the others; the cut-away half of each is a faint ghost), picking leaves out whatever any of them cuts away. **Look:** translucent grey as the world panes (`theme.pane`, `world.paneOpacity`), a little stronger when selected; invisible: fainter and *not pickable* (picking leaves it out); only its icon answers, fading in while the pointer is over the sheet (`ui/sheetchips.ts`). **A face like any other** when not invisible: a click selects it (the click no longer switches its cut), a sketch tool begins on it. **Arrow:** a selected sheet has the push / pull arrow along its normal (`sheet:n`), which moves the plane (nothing is made), with the distance from the origin along the normal as its bubble (typed: field 6). **Move icon** (`sheet:move`, `AppState.sheetMove`): the arrow only, the free mover (arrows along x, y, z, rings, the sphere), the plane across X, across Y, across Z, then back to the free place it left (remembered in the view state). **Menu:** *Plane* (a new sheet that does not cut) and *Cross section* (one that cuts, or puts the selected one away). A new sheet lies through the middle of the part across the axis the camera looks along, or on the flat surface selected.

**Not built:** hatching the cut face where it is also cut by another sheet is done by clipping, not by rebuilding the outline (so a corner where two cuts meet is exact only to the clip); the remembered free place is view state, not saved; sheets as the plane for other tools (a mirror plane, a sweep's plane) are possible now but no tool uses them yet.

**Changed by `in_466`:** no ghost of the cut-away half is left; the sheet's **cut type** (cuts the half it faces, the other half, does not cut: `modeOf`, 0 to 2) has its own icon and **visible / invisible** (an eye) another (`sheet:visible:<ref>`, `Plane.hidden`; an invisible sheet is still able to cut, its edge is a little more visible and its icons fade in on hover); the move orb (the mover's arrows, rings and sphere) **replaces** the arrow along the normal while it is there; a visible sheet lights the blue hover disc under the pointer as every surface does; where a plane lies exactly in a surface the hatch is drawn over it and does not flicker with it (the body is clipped a hair on the cut-away side, the stencil count a hair the other way, the hatch is offset towards the eye); the menu has **one** item, *Plane / cross section* (`view:section`), which always makes a new plane that cuts; the put-away toggle is gone (delete removes a plane). Not checked in the browser against a face exactly in the plane (only a plane a little inside a part and one at the part's edge).

## Rationale

Keeping the part kind and the `section` flag, adding `flip` and `hidden`, means saved files and every existing rule about planes keep working; the view and the picking were the parts that had to learn "more than one".

## Built: the plane's mover has panes and one switch (`in_1129`)
The user asked for the cross section plane to have a more intuitive interface, like the world view's. **Now** (`gizmo.ts` `sectionGizmo`, `control/section.ts`, `control/pointer.ts`): a selected plane has the mover a body has (the arrows along x, y, z, or the rings about them, one icon on the plane goes between the two: it replaces the old five-state move icon), a sphere at the junction, and **three panes** (a small square across each axis beside the junction); a click on a pane lays the plane across that axis where its middle is (the lit pane is the one it lies across); the sheet's own arrow along its normal is there only when it is turned off the axes. **The sphere** is selected by a click (a second lets it go); with it selected a corner, the middle of an edge or a circle, or a face clicked puts the plane's middle there; an edge also turns it across the edge, a flat face lays it on the face (`sheetPlaceAt`). The click on a pane or the sphere is the handle's, not a click on the plane under it. **The cut face** is a darker grey with a bolder hatch (`ui/view.ts`). My choices, for review: the size and place of the panes (a quarter of the mover's length, in the quadrant between the axes), the icon of the move switch (the move and rotate icons), and the sphere staying selected after a placement. The across-X/Y/Z modes of the old icon are gone: the panes do them.

## Built: one helpful bar for the icons of what is selected (`in_1160`)
The icons that stood on the bodies, the sheets and the gear (a body's eye and play button, the icon of its constraints, a sheet's four icons, the gear's list) are one row with the suggested constraints (`ui/suggest.ts`, `interaction/bar.ts`): the icons to the left, the suggested constraints to the right, the constraints themselves keeping their bubbles. It is there for what is selected; its place is by the suggested constraints when there are any, else above the body or by the sheet's corner. **The icon of a body's constraints** (half eye, half lock) is in it only once the body has been double clicked (`AppState.consFor`), until the body is no longer selected. **Kept where they were:** the eye of a body that is hidden and the icons of a sheet that is invisible, which cannot be selected, so they cannot be in the bar (they show while the pointer is over them). The gear's own button is the last icon before the constraints and opens its list under the row. Several bodies selected: one eye that hides them. My choices, for review: that order of icons, the place above a body, and the one eye for several bodies.

## Built: the bar's finish (`in_1177`)
**The constraints icon** is a lock (open while off, closed while on) and is there for a face that is selected as well as for a double clicked body; the constraints it shows go off by themselves when the body or the face is no longer selected (`bodiesOfSelection`, `Frame` and `reduce`). A face's are those of the face, its edges and corners, and the mates and relations that name it (`annotationsOfBodies`). **The holder** has a solid outline; the suggested constraints are in a dashed outline of their own on its right. **A press pops the button** (it grows and settles); a suggestion that is made is no longer on offer and fades out, one that is not stays. **A hidden body's eye** is in the holder too: while the pointer is over a hidden body (or on the way up to it) the holder shows with the eye as its only icon, above the body; a click brings the body back (`ui/bodychips.ts` now only finds the body under the pointer). The invisible sheet's icons are still on the sheet. My choices, for review: how far above the body the holder stands, and that the hidden eye is added to the holder of what is selected when there is one.


## Changed: the bar keeps clear of the pointer (`in_1307`)
The helpful bar stood under the click that selected what it belongs to. It now stands 104 px below its anchor (it was 77) and treats a box round the pointer (90 px each side, 60 above, 70 below) as something in front of it, so it slides out of the way as it does for the ring and the bubbles (`main.ts`, `ui/suggest.ts`). Seen in the browser on three clicks: the bar stood 89 to 112 px away from each.

**Only while it opens (`in_1345`):** the box round the pointer made the bar slide away from a pointer that came to it, so it could not be clicked. Now the box counts only the moment the bar opens or what it belongs to changes (`fresh` in `ui/suggest.ts`): it never opens under the click. Once open it keeps its place (the bubbles' memory holds it while the way is clear), so it can be hovered and clicked. It still slides out of the way of the ring and the bubbles. Not seen in the browser.
