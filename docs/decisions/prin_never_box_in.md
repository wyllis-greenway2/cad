# Never box yourself in

Clean separation of concerns, open registries instead of hardcoded enums, interfaces between layers, and
the ability to change any piece without rewriting its neighbors.

## Rationale
Human-authored: blueprint section 5, principle 5. It is the reason the geometry kernel and the platform
sit behind interfaces.
