# Timing pulleys: the belt dimensions read so far, and the groove shape that is still missing

**Asked (`in_548`):** timing pulleys, after the refusal messages (done). The human agreed (`in_549`) that the AI should ask for network access to read the real tables; a personal environment `cad` allowing `www.pfeiferindustries.com`, `pfeiferindustries.com` and `www.york-ind.com` (and the `www.zajaru.com` the Default had) was approved and made.

**Read from a source (Pfeifer Industries, "Timing Belt Pulley PD and OD" charts, https://www.pfeiferindustries.com, 2025-11-16):** the pitch diameter is pitch × teeth / π, the outside diameter is smaller by twice the pitch line differential U (the distance from the cord to the bottom of the belt tooth). From the metric rows of the charts:
- 2 mm PowerGrip GT / GT2: 20 teeth, PD 12.73, OD 12.22: U = 0.255 (0.254 mm)
- 3 mm HTD: 20 teeth, PD 19.10, OD 18.34: U = 0.38 (0.381 mm)
- 5 mm HTD: 20 teeth, PD 31.83, OD 30.69: U = 0.57 (0.572 mm)
- 0.080" MXL (2.032 mm): 20 teeth, PD 12.94, OD 12.43: U = 0.254 mm
- The T2.5 and T5 charts do not follow PD = pitch × teeth / π (T2.5 10 teeth PD 8.05; T5 10 teeth PD 16.05), so their rule is not taken from this yet.
- The York Industries page is the inch MXL / XL / L standard's text (its groove profile is "formed by the generating tool rack form" of its Table 3, which is an image or a download, not on the page).

**Second try (`in_550`):** the human allowed more hosts; `www.ondrivesus.com`, `sitspa.com`, `www.optibelt.com` and `documents.mrosupply.com` were added to the environment. Read: Ondrives' GT2 pulley stock sheet, SIT's TOP DRIVE HTD catalogue and MRO's 60-groove 5M dimension sheet: all are stock tables (diameters, hubs, lengths, diameter tolerances), none draws the groove or gives its radii; Optibelt's host refuses the connection (403 through the proxy).

**Third try (the human asked to search again):** hosts `sdp-si.com`, `amironic.co.il` and `us.misumi-ec.com` were added with approval. SDP/SI's Technical Section (https://sdp-si.com/D820/PDFS/Technical-Section.pdf, p. T-15, Fig. 19, read as an image) draws the **belt** tooth of each profile with its numbers (inches, mm in brackets): MXL pitch 2.03, tooth height 0.76, belt 1.14 thick, 40 degree flank, 0.13 R; XL pitch 5.08, belt 2.3 thick, 50 degrees, 0.4 R; HTD 3 mm tooth height 1.22, belt 2.41 thick; HTD 5 mm tooth height 2.08, belt 3.81 thick; GT 2 mm tooth height 0.76, belt 1.52 thick; GT3 3 mm tooth height 1.14, belt 2.41; GT3 5 mm tooth height 1.93, belt 3.81; T2.5, T5 and T10 are drawn with their flank angle (40 degrees) and radii (0.2, 0.4, 0.6 R). Its Section 17.4 gives the nominal GT3 **groove** depths: 2 mm 0.76, 3 mm 1.14, 5 mm 1.93, and its Table 18 the pulley outside diameter tolerances. These are belt tooth drawings, not the pulley's rack form: a pulley's groove is the belt's tooth with clearance, and for HTD the clearance is large (the same source says so). Amironic's and Misumi's PDFs list pulleys (backlash, groove, shaft) but not the groove drawing. Also found in a search (a forum and a CAD tutorial quoting a Gates manual, not read at source): HTD 5 mm belt tooth crown radius 1.49, valley radius 0.43.

**Fourth try (the human asked for a search for GT2 2 mm and HTD 5M only; hosts `www.gates.com`, `www.chiefdelphi.com`, `capolight.wordpress.com`, `bbs.homeshopmachinist.net` added with approval):** Gates' own Light Power and Precision manual (https://www.gates.com/content/dam/documents-library/catalogs/light-power-and-precision-manual.pdf) has only the stock sprocket tables (it agrees with Pfeifer: 2MR 20 grooves PD 0.501 in, OD 0.481 in), not the groove form. The Chief Delphi "Timing Pulley Design Tutorial" (https://www.chiefdelphi.com/t/timing-pulley-design-tutorial/383204) draws the pulley groove as the belt tooth: HTD 5 mm: tooth radius 1.49 mm, root fillet 0.43 mm, belt tooth height 2.06 mm, belt 3.80 mm thick, flat between teeth 0.74 mm, cut at the pitch circle; for GT2 it uses the Gates drawing's R1, R2, R3 and b (the numbers are in an image there, not in the text). The Capolight tutorial (https://capolight.wordpress.com/2018/06/16/full-guide-to-creating-a-htd-timing-pulley-in-cad-fusion-360/) gives the same HTD 5 mm belt numbers (pitch 5, tooth height 2.08, belt 3.81, tooth width 3.05, R 1.49, valley R 0.43) and carries a 2020 update from its author that a commenter pointed out that **this belt-derived pulley profile "is not within spec for a HTD timing pulley"**, fine for a quick prototype, risky for long use. The machinist forum page refused the connection (403).

**Fifth: the human sent the Chief Delphi page as a PDF (`in_551`).** It carries a table (Misumi's drawing of the Gates GT belts, with the belt tooth drawn: P, R1, R2, R3, b, H, h, i, PLD, mm): **2GT** P 2, R1 0.15, R2 1.00, R3 0.555, b 0.40, H 1.38, h 0.75, i 0.63, PLD 0.254; **3GT** P 3, R1 0.25, R2 1.52, R3 0.85, b 0.61, H 2.40, h 1.14, i 1.26, PLD 0.381. They hang together: for 2GT the centres of R2 (on the flat, b from the middle) and of R3 (h - R3 below it) are exactly R2 - R3 = 0.445 apart, so the three arcs touch smoothly (3GT within the table's rounding); PLD 0.254 agrees with Pfeifer's chart; h agrees with SDP/SI's GT3 groove depths (0.76, 1.14). **GT2 2 mm and 3GT pulleys were built from it** (`operation/pulley.ts`): the groove is the belt tooth, made `clearance` mm bigger all round. This is still the belt's tooth, not the pulley makers' groove drawing, so it is **to be checked against a real belt**, as the Capolight author warns for HTD. The HTD 5 mm numbers on the same page (R 1.49, root R 0.43, tooth height 2.06, belt 3.80) are not a table of the same kind (the 3.05 mm width and the 0.74 mm flat do not fit a tooth drawn from them), so **HTD 5 mm was not built at first**. (`in_555`) It was then built from the radii (R1 1.49, R2 0.43, height 2.06, PLD 0.572) with straight sides and a clearance of 0.07 mm by default, marked unverified like GT2: see `dec_gear_tool`. HTD 3M and 8M, MXL and the T-series remain unbuilt.

**Missing:** the shape of the groove itself (depth, radii, width) for each belt. No source readable from here gave it (searches returned fragments; the community OpenSCAD generators' hosts are not allowed and are not authoritative). Nothing is written from memory: a belt that does not fit is a failed part.

**Options:** the groove drawings are in the belt makers' design manuals (Gates PowerGrip GT2 and HTD, the ISO 13050 standard), which are not on the hosts allowed so far; the human pastes a table or drawing, or names a host that has one; or build the pulley outside diameter, pitch and pulley rules from the table above with a groove drawn from the belt's tooth height, marked unverified, to be checked against a real belt.

## Rationale

Kept open for HTD 5 mm (and the other belts) and for the check of the GT2 groove against a real belt.

**HTD 3M and 8M (`in_558`).** `www.protorque.net` was added to the `cad` environment with approval. Its HTD datasheet gives the 5M, 8M and 14M belt tooth (height, width, head and foot radius, belt thickness): 8M was built from it (see `dec_gear_tool`); there is no 3M column. Pfeifer's "(3mm HTD)" and "(8mm HTD)" PD and OD charts give the pitch line differential (3M 0.381, 8M 0.686). **Open: the HTD 3M tooth radii** (a Fusion 360 comment quotes 0.306 mm for the small radius, not a source). A pasted Gates or ISO 13050 table, or another host with it, would let 3M be built.

**MXL, XL and T-series, and HTD 3M, searched again (`in_559`, `in_560`).** Hosts `www.synchronous-belts.com`, `cdn.standards.iteh.ai`, `web.optibelt.com`, `cdn.automationdirect.com` and `c-rproducts.com` were added with approval; the automatic safety check then refused further downloads (one of them the free ISO 5294 sample, a copyrighted standard), and Optibelt's host served a script page, not the PDF. Found by search only, not read at source: pulley tooth depths for XL (addendum 0.025", dedendum 0.035") and L (0.045", 0.060") from a CNC tutorial; addendum fillet radii by pitch from synchronous-belts.com in doubtful units. **Skipped for now by the human (`in_560`); on the to-do list** until a groove table (ISO 5294 / the MXL-XL-L pulley standard, Gates, Mädler, or a pasted table) is available.
