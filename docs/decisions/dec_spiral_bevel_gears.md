# Spiral bevel gear tool (accepted in_836; built)

**Proposed:** Gear > Spiral bevel, a bevel gear record with a spiral angle (mean, default 30 degrees) and a hand, its tooth surfaces from the gearing law of Stadtfeld chapter 2 (the crown gear's circular-arc flank enveloped by the pinion, the ring by the pinion), the body a loft of tooth sections cut by the cone-end blank, as `prototypes/bevel-spiral-generated` does. **Limits (measured there):** seven pairs between 16 and 45 teeth mesh to within 4 mm3 of overlap at their place (against 357 to 454 mm3 half a pitch round); refused: a ring cone steeper than about 65 degrees (17/43 fails: the kernel's cone cut), more than 56 teeth in all (the largest that worked is 22/34; 28/42, 30/45 and 25/50, 70 to 75 in all, failed, two by running the kernel out of memory: the true limit is between 56 and 70), shaft angles other than 90 degrees, a profile shift (not tried). The cone cut is fragile (a failed boolean can corrupt the kernel): the tool would try several cone shifts and report failure. **Not proposed:** hypoid (the offset geometry is not read yet).

## Rationale

The prototype shows the pair conjugate in the kernel for the tooth counts above; the limits are what failed. This is my proposal; the human has not approved it, and it waits for the human.

## As built (`in_836`)
Gear > **Spiral bevel** (`tool:spiral`). A spiral bevel gear is a **bevel gear record** with `bevel.spiral` (the mean spiral angle, degrees; default 30, 15 to 45) and `bevel.cone`; its pair (`spiralFor`, `spiral.ts`: the prototype's gearing-law maths rewritten) is worked out from the two tooth counts (the one with fewer teeth the pinion; with equal teeth the partner, which has a direction, the ring gear). Fields: Teeth, Mate teeth, Module (the normal module at the middle of the face), Spiral angle, Backlash (0.05 mm by default), Bore (+ flat, key); fixed: 90 degree shafts, 20 degrees, the face 0.3 of the mean cone distance, the cutter 0.88 of it. Mesh gear, linking (the pair's teeth, spiral angle and backlash follow either gear's box) and re-seating are the bevel gear's, the partner's phase from the pair's place (`meshBevel`: no tooth to snap to, the surfaces are conjugate at any roll). Body (`spiralscene.ts`): a smooth loft through seven planes across the axis (six sides to a tooth: two arcs a flank, one across the tip, one across the root; nine planes and four arcs made a gear in 46 s, over the browser's 60 s limit, this one in 7 to 11 s) intersected with the blank of the two back cones. **Limits, refused with the reason:** pinion under 16 teeth, more than 56 in all, ring over 2.05 times the pinion, angle outside 15 to 45, shafts other than 90 degrees, pressure other than 20. **The kernel's cone cut** fails for some places of the cones and not others, depending on where the gear stands: `SPIRAL.shifts` are tried in turn (`spiralretry.ts`, in `run` and in `refresh`) and the one that works is kept in the gear (`bevel.cone`), so a saved file does not try again. **Measured** (`app/test/spiral.test.ts`, by Mesh gear): 20 / 30 module 3: overlap about 1 mm3 at its place, 379 half a pitch round. **Not built:** hypoid, other shaft angles, a profile shift, a root fillet.

## Limits opened (`in_927`, `prototypes/cone-cut`)
The kernel's cone cut that refused 17 / 43, 25 / 50, 28 / 42 and 30 / 45 no longer fails: after the flank grid's margin (1.5) and the split of the arcs (`splitOf`, `in_888`), the cone retry and the seven sections, **every pair tried made both gears and fitted** (overlap 0 to 6 mm3 at the place): 17 / 34, 17 / 43 (ratio 2.53), 20 / 40, 24 / 36, 25 / 50, 28 / 42, 30 / 45 (module 2.5), 30 / 50 (module 2), 40 / 60 (module 2, 100 teeth in all), each gear in 6 to 42 s here (the browser takes about 1.3 times that; 28 / 42 took 42 s for its ring after cone retries). The limits are now **64 teeth in all** (was 56; 80 first, lowered after the browser run below) and **a ratio of 2.55** (was 2.05); 16 / 48 (ratio 3) makes no tooth outline ("the tooth surfaces make no outline"), a geometric limit, not the kernel's. Tried first, not needed: the boolean's fuzzy tolerance (0.0001 to 0.01) and the oriented box: no change.

## The browser's time (`in_967`)
Measured in Chromium here with the built app (a project file opened, the time the busy notice shows, **both gears** in one job; the limit is 60 s): 20 / 36 24.8 s, hypoid 24 / 40 offset 17 25.1 s, hypoid 20 / 40 offset 20 25.6 s, 17 / 43 28.1 s, 25 / 50 32.8 s, 30 / 45 module 2.5 45.2 s, **28 / 42 56.8 s** (at the limit: its ring takes cone retries). So the total is at most 64 teeth: what was measured up to 60 took 25 to 28 s; 70 and 75 took 33 to 57 s, too near the minute on a slower machine. Other machines may be slower: the time is the gear's cone retries more than its tooth count.

## The kernel's time halved (`in_988`, item 3 of the plan)
**Where the time went:** for a 28 / 42 ring gear (node, 40 s in all): the boolean that cuts the tooth loft with the blank of the two cones 30.8 s (plus 3.5 s of its check), the revolve 2.4 s, the loft 1 s; no cone retries. Not the retries, and not the boolean's own options (oriented boxes, fuzzy value, non-destructive: no change). **What it depends on is the degree of the loft's surfaces:** the kernel's smooth loft through seven sections is a surface of degree up to six, and a boolean with it takes more than twice as long as with a degree three (the same ring: 5 sections 15.6 s, 6 sections 27 s, 7 sections 40 s; fewer sections lose the fit: 5 sections overlapped 135 mm3 for 17 / 43 and 28 / 42, 6 sections 118 mm3 for 17 / 43). **Done:** the loft step takes `maxDegree` (3: `graph.ts`, `interpreter.mjs` `limitedLoft`, `ThruSections.SetMaxDegree`), set for the spiral and hypoid gear (`spiralscene.ts`) and the face gear's. **Measured:** the 28 / 42 ring 16 s (was 40), the pair's fit as good or better (17 / 43 0.8 mm3, 28 / 42 0.4, 20 / 36 offset 12 0.7, 20 / 40 offset 20 1.1; the half-pitch overlaps as before), the volumes the same to the fifth digit, the rendered teeth the same; the face gear 16 s (was 22.6). **In the browser (both gears, one job):** 28 / 42 33.1 s (was 56.8), 30 / 45 module 2.5 34.2 s (was 45.2), 25 / 50 30.9 s (was 32.8), 40 / 60 module 2 (100 teeth) 42.7 s. **The total is at most 80 teeth again** (it was cut to 64 after the first browser run, above).
