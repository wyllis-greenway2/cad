# One way to do each thing in the interface (the audit, and the six steps built)

`in_662` asked how to make an operation's option buttons usable in game mode, and for an audit of what can be unified: notifications, refusal rules, buttons and bubbles, the menu, apply and cancel, the operation's options, the mobile bar, "Add more", and the many kinds of X Y Z orbs. The audit (what the code did, read from the code) and the AI's proposal; the human approved the order and each step (`in_663` to `in_668`) and then asked that this become a decision (`in_669`). All six steps are built (see Progress).

## What is there today
- **Game mode:** the crosshair already presses whatever page element is under it (`dom-input.ts` `underCrosshair`, hover included), and Alt frees the real pointer. What cannot be pressed is what stands at a fixed place on the screen (the operation pill at the top, the option chips, the undo bar): the crosshair is always at the middle and the view moves, not the chip, so it can never be aimed at one.
- **Messages (about nine ways):** the status line (`session.message`), the refusal chip with "Delete constraint to proceed" (`flash`), the helper bubble on an arrow, the hint icons, the busy sign, the suggestion row, the similar chip, the pressed-and-held tooltips, and the number chips.
- **Pills and chips (about fifteen files):** `toolchip`, `chip`, `facekindchip` (also used for size kinds and loft kinds), `similarchip`, `addchip`, `cutparts`, `sheetchips`, `undobar`, `refusalchip`, `suggest`, `resetview`, `modeswitch`, each with its own look code except those sharing `look.ts` (`PILL`, `choiceRow`).
- **Apply and cancel:** an operation applies with Enter, a right click on blank space, or the check of the top pill, and cancels with Esc or the cross (`doApply`, `pointer.ts`); a **sketch tool** stays on until a right click or the cross and its check only means "done"; a **repeat** applies as an operation does; **picking** (an operation chosen first) applies on the same keys; **cut** has its own pill ("Cut"); the **mover** has "Orient the mover"; the **dimension** operation has no pill at all (`frame.ts` `confirm`); on touch the check is hidden for an operation (`toolchip.ts`, `in_286`: "tap away to apply"), which is why a rotation extrude on a phone has no check. Chip choices (push pull kind, size kind, loft kind, select-similar rules) take effect at once.
- **Refusals:** a change that would break a constraint is refused with the flashing and the "Delete constraint to proceed" chip only on the paths that go through the refusal (`flash.retry`); other paths just say a sentence in the status line.
- **Menus:** the ring's categories and operations are always there (dimmed); since `in_661` the relations that fit the selection stay too, but the relations that do not fit its kinds are absent, so the Constraints list is empty (one placeholder) until something is selected.
- **X Y Z handles:** five handle kinds (`arrow`, `double`, `arc`, `ball`, `scale`) in about nine families: the mover (arrows `move:`, rings `rot:`, orb `pivot`, and the same with `piv:` when the orb is selected), the revolve's orb, rings, position arrows and spiral arrows (`rev:*`, `piv:*`), the mirror's orb and arrows, the linear repeat's count and distance arrows with an orb, the circular repeat's pivot arrows, the scale arrows, the push pull arrow, the fillet's double arrow, the section plane's arrows and rings and the sheet's own arrow, and the joint handles.

## Proposed (and approved, step by step)
1. **Game mode:** do not put buttons in the world. Give the operation pill the options too (below), and make **Tab and Shift+Tab step through the options** of the open operation (each step chooses it, so the chip's choice is made without aiming), with Enter / right click / Esc as now. This works in game mode, on a keyboard alone, and needs one mechanism, not one per chip. Tab currently moves between typed number fields; the fields would take another key (for example the up and down arrows, which already change a value) or Tab would step options only when the operation has options.
2. **One operation bar** (the top pill, drawn by one component): operation icon, its options (the chips that sit beside the number today), Apply (check) and Cancel (cross) **for every operation, tool, repeat, picking, cut and mover state**, on every device; on a phone it moves to the bottom, joined with undo and redo. Apply stays Enter, right click on blank space, or the check; cancel Esc or the cross; a sketch tool keeps "stays on until applied or put away".
3. **One notification path:** every message and refusal goes through one `notify({text, kind, anchor?, action?})` in the interaction layer and one renderer (a line at the bottom, or a bubble at an anchor, with an optional button such as "Delete constraint to proceed"); the helper, hint and status line become kinds of it. **One refusal rule:** any change refused because of a constraint offers "Delete constraint to proceed", whichever tool made it.
4. **One bubble and one button:** a single bubble component (icon, number, options, cross) and a single round button look (`look.ts` grows to hold it), so the files above shrink to descriptions.
5. **Menu:** every constraint and mate the table knows is in the Constraints list always, dimmed unless it fits the selection (about forty, grouped by what they act on; the ring would need sub-rings), so the ring's shape never depends on the selection.
6. **Mobile:** "Add more" leaves the screen and becomes a mobile-only bubble in the finger menu (it is already `select:add` in the Select category; only the pill goes).
7. **Handles:** reduce to three kinds (an arrow with a tip, a ring, an orb), one family per job (move, turn, size, count), and one drawing and one drag code; the revolve, mirror, repeat and mover would be the same family with a different set of axes.

**Suggested order (smallest first, each testable alone):** 6, then 2 with 1, then 3, then 5, then 4, then 7 (the handles are the most work and touch the picking and drag tests).

## Progress
- **Step 1 done (`in_663`, the human approved the order and asked to start with Add more):** the "+ Add more" offer is gone; **Add mode** is a bubble in the Select ring only when the menu was opened by a finger (a long press carries `touch`; a mouse has Shift), and while adding is on it stays in every menu (it reads "Stop adding"). The pill remains only to say adding is on ("Adding: tap to add or remove · Done"), which is the one thing a finger cannot otherwise see. Next: the operation bar with Tab stepping (2 and 1).
- **Step 2 done (`in_664`):** the top pill is now **the operation bar** (`ui/toolchip.ts`): it shows the operation's icon, its **options** (what a push / pull of a face does, a fillet's way of running, a loft's way of joining: the three separate chips are gone, `interaction/options.ts` lists them), and the check and cross for every operation on every device (a finger's check was hidden before). On a finger it sits at the bottom with undo and redo in it (the separate undo bar is gone). **Tab and Shift+Tab step through the choices between ways** (each step chooses, the one Tab is on is outlined); the loft's switches (Split, Start, Reverse) are left to the pointer. **Where Tab already moved between numbers** (a varying fillet, a two-distance chamfer, the spiral's angle and advance, a figure's two sizes) it still does, with Shift+Tab stepping the options in the fillet, and **the left and right arrows go between numbers everywhere**. **Not in the bar yet:** the dimension operation (it opens on every selection of a drawn part: a bar there would be noise), the Select similar chip, the Cut chips and the sheet chips (they sit at things in the scene).
- **Step 3 done (`in_665`):** **one notification description and one renderer.** `interaction/notice.ts` turns the frame into one list of `Notice`s (kinds: `status`, `refusal`, `help`, `busy`; each with its words, where it stands, and an optional button that sends an intent), `Frame.notices`; `ui/notices.ts` draws all of them (the bottom line, the refusal pill beside the constraint's bubble, the help on an arrow, the working sign) and reports a button press the same way for every kind. `refusalchip.ts`, `helper.ts`, `busy.ts` and the page's own writes to the status line are gone. A refusal's words are no longer also in the bottom line (they were twice). **One refusal rule:** a change refused because constraints hold it offers "Delete constraint to proceed" on every path where the constraints are known: the body that cannot move because a constraint ties its sketch to something that stays, and a sketch side that a constraint holds, now offer it (before, they only named the constraint). **Not changed, said plainly:** a change refused because the kernel could not make a step made from it, the standing mates at odds (`atOdds`) and an undo or redo that would leave mates at odds still name what stopped them without a button: deleting a constraint would not mend the first, and the last two are mates, whose delete needs its own command, which I did not build; say if the button should be offered there too. The hint icons, suggestions and the touch tooltips are controls, not notices.
- **Step 4 done (`in_666`):** the Constraints ring lists **every** constraint and mate (`interaction/constraintlist.ts`, built from the relations table; `actions.ts` `relationKinds`), in eleven sub-rings by what they apply to; the ring is the same shape whatever is selected; what does not fit is dimmed with "select two lines first" and the like. A constraint that fits several kinds (Distance, Tangent) is in each group and lit only in the one the selection is. Labels follow the two-word rule (Point-line, Faces axes, Axis-face).
- **Changed (`in_832`..`in_836`, the human: "isnt tangent just tangent?"): the Constraints ring is one flat list of what a person means, each once.** The eleven sub-rings by selection are gone (they listed Tangent three times, Distance three times, On circle twice, and had a name for the same idea in 2D and in 3D). The ring now has **Horizontal, Vertical, Parallel, Perpendicular, Angle, Coincident, On, Tangent, Concentric, Equal, Dimension, Fix** and Hard body (13 buttons, no ring inside it; `CONSTRAINT_ENTRIES` in `constraintlist.ts`). Each entry stands for several of the relations table's and the mates' ids (Parallel is the relation of two lines, the mate of two flat faces and of two axes; Perpendicular also covers a mate of an axis to a face; Angle also the arc angle; Coincident also Flush; Concentric also Coaxial; Dimension is diameter, radius, length and distance) and makes the first of them that the selection fits (`resolveEntry`); an entry nothing fits is dimmed and says what to select, and pressed it waits for it (`dec_menu_picking`). The ring is still the same shape whatever is selected. **Not built:** Horizontal and Vertical on a face or an axis (a plane level or upright against the world): the mates join two bodies, and a mate with the world's up direction as its other side touches `mates.ts`, `matesolve.ts` and `mate.ts`; Tangent between bodies (a cylinder on a plane) does not exist. Tests: `menutips.test.ts`.
- **Step 5 done (`in_667`):** the shapes everything is made of are in `ui/look.ts`: `roundButton` (one round button: icon, size, tone light / dark / danger / accent, faded when not live, the same hover) with `onPress`, `bubbleCss` (the white number pill), `textButton` (words, for pages and dialogs), and the existing `PILL` and `choiceRow`. Now made from them: the ring's bubbles, the suggestion buttons, the sheet's four controls, the mode switch, the hint marks, the operation bar's check, cross, undo and redo, the constraint bubbles, the number bubble, the dialog's buttons and the settings page's buttons. **Not converted:** the X Y Z and ⋯ buttons (`controls.ts`: their colour and border change with the view), the Cut parts chips (a pill with a plus or minus and a letter) and the world orb; the pills that carry choices (`PILL`) were already one look. The look of each is as it was, except that every round light button now also lights its fill on hover, and the ring's did the edge only.
- **Step 6 done (`in_668`), the last of the order:** **three kinds of handle, made one way** (`interaction/gizmo.ts`). `HandleKind` is now `arrow | ring | orb`: what was a double arrow is an arrow with `both` (a head at each end, pulled from its middle: the push / pull, the fillet's size, the sheet's normal) and what was a scale handle is an arrow with `ratio` (its value is how many times as far along it the pointer is as where it was taken). One constructor each (`arrowAlong`, `arrowBoth`, `axisArrows`, `orbAt`, `ring`) builds every handle of every family (the mover, the revolve's orb, rings, place and spiral arrows, the mirror, the two repeats, scale, the push / pull, the fillet, the sheet, the joint); one `startDrag` and one `dragTo` have one branch per kind (and the ratio of an arrow); the view draws the three kinds with one loop (`ui/view/gizmo.ts`). **Not changed, said plainly:** the families themselves (which ids a move, a turn, a size and a count have, and what a drag of each does in `control/handles.ts`) are as they were: they are chosen by the id (`move:x`, `rev:adv:z`, `cnt:y`), which the tests, the touch helper and the number bubbles all use, so merging them is a larger change that needs the human's word. **All six steps of the order are done.**
- **Game mode check (`in_669`):** in Chromium with the pointer really locked (`pointerLockElement` set), Space starts game mode, Tab and Shift+Tab step Move, Extrude, Extend (each step chooses it), and a typed 3 with Enter applies the push / pull as an extend step: the options are reachable in game mode without aiming. The crosshair still cannot press a screen-fixed button, by design.
- **Left unmerged (the human's word, `in_669`):** the handle families by id (`move:`, `piv:`, `rev:*`, `mir:`, `lin:`, `cnt:`, `scale:`): they are the ids the touch helper, the number bubbles and the tests use; revisit only if a new family is painful to add.
- **No loose ends are left open.**

- **The bar chips (`in_670`, the human took the recommendation):** **Select similar and Cut are in the operation bar.** Select similar's rules are switches in the bar (with a check that puts the rules away and keeps the selection; the separate chip is gone); Cut's parts (kept or tossed) and its join are switches in the bar (its letters at the parts in the scene stay). **Tab now lands on every option** (it chooses a way between ways as it lands on it, and only lights a switch or an action), **Shift+Enter flips the switch the bar's focus is on**, so both are reachable in game mode. The **sheet chips and the dimension operation stay where they are**: they belong to a thing in the scene, and the dimension operation opens on every selection of a drawn part.

- **Mates at odds (`in_671`, the human took the recommendation):** a change refused because it would leave standing mates unable to hold at once now offers "Delete constraint to proceed": it deletes the standing mates that are there and makes the change, as one step. **Not for an undo or a redo** (they do not go through this refusal: deleting a mate would turn an undo into something else); **not for a kernel step that cannot be made** (deleting a constraint would not mend it), which keep naming what stopped them.

- **The round controls (`in_672`, the human took the recommendation):** the X Y Z and ⋯ buttons (`controls.ts`), the Cut chips at the parts (`cutparts.ts`) and the world orb (`worldorb.ts`) **keep their own looks**: their colour and border change with the view (facing, the selection ring) or they carry a letter, a plus or a minus, so they are not a plain round button. Fold them in only if `roundButton` grows a state for what they show.

- **The browser pass (`in_673`):** Chromium, the real app. *Cut:* the bar shows "A kept, B kept, A∩B kept, Joined"; Tab then Shift+Enter tossed A (the focus stayed on it); Enter made one `combine` step and one body (x 5 to 15, as it should be). It showed that **the old "Kept parts: Joined / Separate" pill was still there beside the bar's own Joined switch: the pill is gone** (`cutparts.ts` keeps only the plus / minus controls at the parts). *Mates at odds:* **the page found a gap the unit test could not:** in the browser the kernel answers later, and the refusal is made in `refresh`, not in `run`, so the first button never appeared; `refresh` now carries the retry too, and then "Delete constraints to proceed" showed beside the red mates and pressing it left one mate (the new one) and no message. *The refusal pill* is wide (it has no single bubble to stand beside when two mates are named) and cuts its words with an ellipsis; it works and I left it.

- **The locked-length pill and the phone pass (`in_674`):** *The pill* (a side that a locked length holds, push / pull typed 50): the words and "Delete constraints to proceed" show at the top, the locked edge flashes red, and the button leaves the body 0 to 50 with the lock gone and no message; it has no single bubble to stand beside, so it is at the top centre. *The late-answer path of the mates button* now has a test (`matesoddslag.test.ts`, with a kernel that answers when told; it fails without the fix). *A phone* (390 x 780, touch emulation, the long press stood in for by the menu intent that it sends): the finger's ring has Add mode under Select and it turns adding on; inches typed into a bubble show "1 in" and make the step 25.4 mm; the settings page lays out on one column. **It found two faults, fixed:** (1) the "Adding: tap to add or remove · Done" pill stood under the operation bar and was hidden by it: it is at the top left now; (2) **the press that opened the settings page clicked on the page**: on a touch screen the click that ends a press arrives on what is then under the finger, so opening Settings changed "A scroll" to Zooms: the page and the dialogs ignore clicks for 450 ms after they open (`guardOpenClicks`, `look.ts`).

## Rationale

The interface had grown one way of doing each thing per feature (nine ways to show a message, fifteen pill files, four ways to finish an operation, a menu that changed shape). Each step puts one description and one renderer where there were several, so a new feature adds a description, not a widget; and each is reachable from the keyboard, which is the only way into the operation bar in game mode.
