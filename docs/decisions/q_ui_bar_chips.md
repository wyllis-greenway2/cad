# Which of the remaining chips belong in the operation bar?

Not in the bar yet: the **dimension operation** (it opens on every selection of a drawn part, so a bar there would be noise), **Select similar** (its rule boxes), the **Cut** chips (they stand at the parts they act on) and the **sheet** chips (they stand at the plane). **Recommendation:** Select similar and Cut join the bar (they are modes with options, and unreachable in game mode today); the sheet chips and the dimension operation stay where they are, since they belong to a thing in the scene.

## Rationale

A loose end of `dec_ui_unification`, left on purpose and recorded so it is decided and not forgotten.
