# One tier, everything included

$5/month or $49/year, a single tier with no feature gating, and a 14-day full-featured trial. STL and
3MF export are never restricted. A team tier (about $25/month for 5 seats) is a possible later addition
with individual pricing staying at $5/month.

## Rationale
Human-authored: blueprint sections 4 and 20 (fair pricing as positioning; volume over extraction). Not
revisited since the blueprint. Things to weigh before launch: free competitors (Fusion personal use,
Onshape free), community dislike of subscriptions, and app-store fees on mobile.
