# Sketching with a finger: the same clicks as the mouse, plus helpers

The human found sketching on a phone hard (`in_283`) and asked for it to work the way it does with a mouse, with a few extras for a finger. Built as a first try, all provisional until used on a phone:

- **Sketching with a finger (`in_288`, replaces the hold-first flow of `in_286`; no check icon, `in_286`):** a finger has no hover, so the second point is *held* where it can be seen and the hints a mouse's hover gives (on line, on circle, tangent, level, snaps) show on it. The **first tap sets the first point right where it is** (a vertex: a ring with a dot) and the **next point is already there**, up and to the right of it (an arc, after its ends, holds its bow beside the chord). **Each point has its own finger-drag icon**, joined to it by a dotted line (`in_287`): drag the start icon to move the first point (it takes the hints too: `movePoint`), drag the finish icon to move the held point (`hover`). **A tap in free space puts the scissors away** unless it is over a part that can be cut (`in_292`); likewise an operation that is open with no arrow chosen: a tap on an arrow selects it and a tap anywhere else is a right click (it applies and ends the operation). **A tap anywhere else sets the point held where it is**; when that finished the figure it is made and **the tool is put away with it** (`in_289`: a tap that applies also ends the tool; an arc goes on to its bow first), so the next figure starts by choosing the tool again. **A circle:** the held point's icon sets the radius and the centre's icon moves the whole circle at that radius; **an arc once its bow is held:** its ends' icons move the whole curve and the bow's icon sets the curve (`in_289`); a rectangle's or line's first icon moves only that corner. **A typed size and Enter on a finger apply it and put the tool away too** (`in_290`; with a mouse the tool stays on). **A drawn point, straight line or circle that is selected has a drag icon** (a finger cannot take hold of a corner or an edge): its proxy drag begins on the part, so the point is moved, the whole line is moved, the circle's size is changed, as with a mouse (`in_290`). **A sign button (±) stands by every number bubble on a phone** because its number keyboard has no minus (an inward offset, a move back; `in_292`). **The drag icons are kept clear of the suggestion row and the constraint bubbles** (they are laid out round them). **The size bubble can be tapped into** with a finger (on a mouse the pointer passes over it): the keyboard opens, a typed number and Enter set the size. So tap, tap makes a rectangle or circle (three for an arc). One finger on empty space always orbits; two fingers pan and zoom. The trim tool has one step: a tap is a click. Built as the intents `tapAim` and `movePoint` (controller `doTapAim`, `doMovePoint`); the input layer only says "tap while a tool is on" and "this icon was dragged".
- **Operations that are dragged (`in_284`, `in_285`):** Move, Turn, Scale, push / pull, extrude, fillet / chamfer, the arrows of a repeat: a tap selects the arrow or ring; the finger-drag icon then drags it exactly as the mouse would (the same `orbit` and `dragEnd` intents). A tap on another arrow selects that; **a tap anywhere else is a right click**: an open operation is applied; with nothing open (an arrow only selected) it is an ordinary click. No check icon here either: the pill at the top for an open operation shows only the cross on a touch screen (`in_286`). It is one mechanism in the input layer (`TouchHelper`, `Frame.touchHelper`): the controller is told nothing about fingers.
- **A long press is a right click** and is told what is under it by looking at the point (a finger has no hover): a sketch tool chosen from the ring turns the view straight to the plane or face there, as with a mouse.
- **Undo and redo are always on the screen** (bottom left) once a touch has been used; a mouse has the keys and the menu.
- **The tool chip at the top:** once a figure is made (and none is half drawn) its cross becomes a **check** that puts the tool away (it is "done", not a cancel); the check flashes green and fades at each figure made, and when an operation opens (the one with apply and cancel), to show it is there to press. Mid-figure the cross cancels that figure.

Not done: the touch icons are a first drawing (the finger-drag icon especially); a drag that begins on a corner of a drawing has no helper yet; not tried on a real phone.

## Rationale

`prin_input_agnostic` asks that nothing exists only on one device. The long-press menu, the sketch-on-a-face rule and the apply gesture were reachable on a mouse through hover and a right click; on touch they were missing or different. The behaviour is in the shared controller, not in the screen code, so the mouse and the finger take the same path; only the extra helpers (drag handle, apply icon, undo bar) belong to touch.
