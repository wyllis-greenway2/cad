
## Built: regions that touch are one figure when extruded together (`in_1306`)
Selected together, regions that share a side (the two halves a line across a ring makes of it) are one figure for an extrude, a push or a cut: their shared sides are taken away and the outline that is left is followed round (the biggest loop is the outside, the others are holes) in `mergedFigures` (`interaction/operations.ts`). Before, each region was a step of its own, and the second had to meet the faces the first one left, which the kernel refused ("kernel returned an invalid solid"): only one half was cut. A group whose outline cannot be followed (a corner where more than two sides meet) is left as separate figures. Test `ringhalves.test.ts` with the user's model (`fixtures/ring-through.cad.json`).
