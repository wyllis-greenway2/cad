Marks: the simple rule set on one scene (a bubble anywhere free, an arrow to its spot, parents light on hover)

Pick what is selected. Every mark is a small bubble in the nearest free place with a thin dashed arrow to the exact spot it is about (pale until you point at it, left out when the bubble is already beside its spot). Pointing lights the parts it names (strong amber) and what it would also move or the line a named vertex ends (pale amber). Yellow is an offer: its arrow joins the two named parts in the direction they move, and what else would move is drawn paler. A length and a circle size keep their dashed dimension line with the bubble on it. Nothing here touches the real app.
