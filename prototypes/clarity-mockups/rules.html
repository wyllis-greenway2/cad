Marks: the draft rule set on one scene (quiet, in the drawing, loud on hover)

Pick what is selected. Marks are quiet until you point at one: then its parts light up amber, what it measures or would move is drawn, and a sentence says it. Yellow is an offer; its arrows show only on hover. Nothing is a pill; marks stand where the constraint is about, step aside when they collide (a thin leader then points back), and fan out around a junction. At a small zoom a mark whose part is tiny shrinks to a dot. Nothing here touches the real app.
