# progressive (prototype, `in_908`)

**Meant to learn:** whether a model can be made in pieces (a group of steps built alone, in any order) and give the same bodies as the whole model: the claim that the safe-interaction rule of `q_progressive_load` rests on.

**Run (from `app/`):** `node --test ../prototypes/progressive/harness.test.ts` (`PROJECTS=a,b` names fixtures; `--test-name-pattern=assemblies` for the made assemblies only).

**Method.** The whole model is built from its scene; its steps are grouped (a step that uses another's body, or whose bodies touch another's by their boxes, joins its group; any step of another kind than a body maker, or one that makes no body of its own (it joins or cuts), makes everything one group: conservative); each group is built alone (the other steps taken out of the scene, the kernel's kept answers cleared) and its bodies compared with the whole model's: volume to 1e-9, box to 1e-6 mm, and the face names; the groups are then built in three other orders.

**Learned:** of 70 fixtures only the two grids are of several groups once the pattern is seen to use its source (the grouping needed that: the first version took the pattern for independent and built it without its source: 162 differences, all of that one mistake); the others are single bodies with modifying steps, which are one group by the rule. Of five assemblies made here (twelve boxes apart; boxes and gears apart; two boxes touching; two overlapping; gears meshing with a box apart) **17 groups built alone and in 6 other orders: 17 bodies, no difference**; the touching and the overlapping boxes were seen to be one group. Groups built alone are also quicker in all than the whole for gears (678 ms for 630) only because nothing is shared: the saving is in the **order and the pieces**, not in the work.

**Not tried:** groups that include modifying steps (fillets of a body of the group): the grouping would have to know which body a step changes (it does not: a fillet names an edge by its place); revolves, lofts and sweeps in assemblies; a body that moves; random edits in the middle (the fuzz of `q_progressive_load`).

## Version 2 (`in_912`): parts with modifying steps, and what the page can know without the kernel

**Added:** assemblies of boxes with their tops rounded, a hole, one slid; ten fixtures of several bodies; for every step that changes a body (a fillet, a push, a move, a turn, a scale) **what it changes is found by taking it out** (the truth), and a **rule the page could use without the kernel** is tried against that truth: a body named by the step (`params.body`, `b/x_..`) is the body; a place (`b/body/e@x,y,z~..` an edge's points, `b/body/f@..` with `px,py,pz`) is the bodies whose boxes hold it; anything not understood is every body before it; bodies tied by a relation (a mate holds a part of one to a part of another, through the sketch the part belongs to), hard bodies, and bodies whose boxes meet (a moved body's taken where it began and where it ends) are one thing.

**Learned**
- **A bug found and fixed:** a body moved among others lost the names of the faces that stand across the motion (`x_s_004:side:..s1` and `s3` became `x_s_007:0` and `1`), because the motion was told the body's name and a face's name is not a body's name, so no name was moved at all. Alone (one body) it was right, so only the harness could see it. `facenames.mjs` `carried` now moves the names inside the box where the body stood (`tf.box`) and leaves the others; `test/movenames.test.ts` (fails without the fix).
- Parts with fillets and a hole: the step changes exactly the one body it names by place; **groups built alone and in other orders give the same bodies, volumes, boxes and face names** (V2: 7 groups, 9 other orders, 11 bodies, no difference; fixtures: 13 groups, 18 orders, 19 bodies, no difference).
- **The rule, against the truth, on 65 modifying steps:** 10 of 10 in the made assemblies; in the fixtures 38 of 55 named every body the step changes, 4 said "every body before it" (safe), and **13 missed a body**: one fixture (`cylinder-grow-in_295-before`), in which a body moved many times meets another at places it passes through (a join made at the step where they meet), which the boxes at the start and at the end do not show.
- A move step removed changes nothing in its own body's final place (the sketch it carries is moved as well: `follow`), so "what it changes" for a move must come from the body it names, not from taking it out.

**So:** a page-side rule can attribute fillets, holes, pushes, moves of named bodies and patterns, but **cannot yet prove independence when bodies are moved through each other's places**; for that the ghost must keep the boxes of every body at every step (the path), or such a project must be built as one group.
